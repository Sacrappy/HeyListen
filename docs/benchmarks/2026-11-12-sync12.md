## What costs the frames

### 1. #2419 (the fence wait covers every part of a split submission): about -10 to -20 fps
- **Readback waits:** the CPU's readbacks of GPU results wait 6.1-7.0 ms each (60-70 of every 3000 readbacks), against
  0.01-0.02 ms on main. They now wait for the submission's last recording, not its first part's.
- **Profile:** a sampled ModuleSystemWorker1 spends its time in `rx_nvn_invalidate -> invalidate -> wait_recorded ->`
  the driver's wait.
- **Thread load (`HOS_PROFILE`):** the module workers go from ~27% of a core to ~45-50% each, and the GPU idles between
  recordings for ~9-10 ms a frame.
- **The measurement switch:** `RX_VK_VALUE_REC_FIRST=1` gives back the first part's mapping. The readback waits drop to
  0.12-0.18 ms, and fps comes back to 67.9 walking (+12) and 69-72 standing (+13-19).
- **Why it waits for the last part:** each part of a split submission records its own write-backs (`maybe_split` and
  `occ_sync` call `memory_end_submission`). A readback of a range written back in part k needs part k, not the last part.
  The old mapping returned after part 1, which can be before part k (upstream's flashes on iGPUs). The new one waits for
  everything.
- **The fix it needs:** the precise wait is for the recording that holds that range's write-back. That needs the
  write-back registrations (`wb_pages_note`) to carry the recording, not the submission value. Not done here.

### 2. The rest: about -10 fps with #2419's mapping off, not isolated
- **Not the anisotropy:** 16x costs ~3 fps standing. HeyListen's default is the game's own again.
- **Not the step:** `HOS_FPS_STEP=0` (the game stepped by the frames' own time above 60 fps) changes nothing.
- **Not the compaction:** `RX_VK_COMPACT=0` was slower in its one run.
- **Pipeline skip:** `RX_VK_PIPE_SKIP=2` (+3) is inside the spread.
- **Renderer side:** the replay itself costs the same on both builds: dry pass, segments, capture 0.26-0.30 ms a submit,
  the same 3036 draws standing.
- **Virtual cores:** the vcores line shows about twice as many core takes and waits a 5 s window on the sync build
  (433k takes / 92k waits, mean wait 43 us, against 186k / 37k, 22 us on main), with 0 escapes. Upstream changed the
  virtual cores: #2387 and #2302 (an escaped thread takes a core again), #2127 (escape at 2 s instead of 250 ms). Its
  sleeps and waits changed too: #2263 sleep_away, WaitAny without checkpoints. Not bisected.
- **Next:** a `HOS_PROFILE` / `HOS_SAMPLE` comparison of `sead::MainThread` and the module workers with `VALUE_REC_FIRST=1`
  on both builds. Main's exe has no map here, so its game-thread stacks cannot be named.
