# TICK-058: the game's chain, its hand-offs, and the main thread's fence poll (2026-11-12)

## Setup
- Ryzen 7 7800X3D (8 cores, 16 threads), RTX 5070, Windows 11; Market save; `run_bench.py` 110 s, `--warmup-s 66`,
  `--fps-limit 360`, frame generation off, windowed. Harness (build/tick058, not in git): `run.py` (one run; the saves checked
  and no game left after every run), `ab.py` (interleaved screen, one binary, variants as environment), `conf.py` (the confirm:
  rounds 4-6 in reverse order, scenes too), `sample.py` (`HOS_SAMPLE_ALL` + `HOS_SAMPLE_NS` with a `HOS_JOBTRACE` window).
  Traces: `HOS_JOBTRACE` (88-91 or 90-93 s), `tools/bench/wakelat.py`, `critpath.py`, `chainb.py`, `frameline.py`.
- Binaries: `bin/v1` (branch a3c9d97, the first fast poll), `bin/v3` (the fast poll with the readback wait, committed after);
  in each, base = no environment = main 0db10b3's behaviour (TICK-054's defaults; every TICK-056 and
  TICK-058 switch off).
- fps / 1% low / p99 ms; frame prep = the Presentation Thread's replay time a frame (ms, the log's `submit`).

## Measurements (castle-move traces, base)
- Wake latency, every sleeping hand-off (297 a frame): p50 6.4 us, p90 10.1, p99 75. Main thread 15.8 a frame at 6.2 us.
- The game chain's critical path: hand-off wakes 0.21-0.23 ms a frame, their virtual-core waits 0.02-0.13, lone virtual-core
  waits 0.07-0.18, poll lag 0.03-0.09: ~0.3-0.5 ms of a 15.6-15.9 ms traced frame.
- The main thread's nvnSyncWait first looks (2 a frame): 1.42 ms a frame, 1.41 of it waiting for the renderer's `sync_order_mu`
  (held for whole replays); with `RX_VK_FENCE_POLL_FAST=1` 0.02 ms, all answered "signalled" in both.
