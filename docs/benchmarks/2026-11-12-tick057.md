### Mirror churn
VRAM sits at 84-90% of the driver's allowance (6.1-6.5 of 7.25 GB, of which 0.7-1.3 GB is free space inside VMA's blocks).
Past 80% (TICK-023's rule) every mirror idle 600 submissions goes: in 40 s of the walk 111 went by that rule and 101 of them
(1.8 GB) were made again within 6000 submissions, each a 16 MB copy from guest memory (17 ms profiled).

Notes:
- The trust switches of TICK-016 are inert while a parallel replay records (`par_active`), which is most of the frame since
  TICK-054: s57a measured almost nothing of them.
- `RX_VK_MIRROR_STICKY=1`: under 16 mirrors given back a run instead of ~110; gpu_up_ms and the tail do not move, nor fps.
- `RX_VK_BARRIER_DEDUPE_PAR=1`: ~110 more full barriers a frame left out inside segments (465 -> ~400 recorded): repeats on an
  idle GPU, which cost nothing measurable.
- `RX_VK_TEST_BARRIER_DROP=1` leaves out the recordings' pass-start, dispatch and the game's own barriers (~330 -> 97 a frame;
  the uploads', write-backs' and segments' edges stay). The picture may be wrong; it is a bound. Every drop run is above every
  base run: +2.5 fps (+8.7%), gpu_ms -2.8 ms, late wait -2.3 ms, the tail -2 ms. The barrier drains are the one engine-side
  GPU cost that shows, and a GPU cut shows nearly 1:1 in the late wait.
- `RX_VK_BARRIER_TRUST=1` (t57c): ~140 dispatch barriers and ~95 pass-start barriers a frame left out, ~20 pass starts made
  attachment-only (src/dst the attachment stages); `RX_VK_GAME_BARRIER_NARROW=1`: ~88-100 of the game's 0x12 barriers a frame
  wait in the fragment stages only. Recorded as counted, yet gpu_ms (19.3-19.6) and the late wait (7.3-7.8) do not move: on this
  driver a barrier with a narrower destination drains like a full one, and the pass-start and dispatch barriers the trust leaves
  out mostly followed a barrier already in flight. The 2.8 ms of the drop bound sit in the game's own barriers' drains, which
  only leaving them out removes (not safe: the console needs them as much).
- No switch was adopted; no confirm and no validation run (nothing to adopt).
