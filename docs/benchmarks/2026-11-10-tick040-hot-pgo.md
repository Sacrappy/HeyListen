# TICK-040: hot set, PGO profile and back-edge polls at the Market (2026-11-10)

## Setup
- Hardware: Ryzen 7 7800X3D, RTX 5070 12 GB, Windows 11.
- Builds: worktree `feature/TICK-040-hot-pgo` (main 4eb623c), built in the dev-build work folder (`%LOCALAPPDATA%\HookshotBuilder`) with the builder's tools (llvm-mingw of v0.1.0-rc1), 8 jobs. Shaders not re-prepared (no stamp input changed).
- Runs: `tools/bench/run_bench.py`, windowed, 110 s, `--fps-limit 360`, frame generation off (options file), audio off; cut at 66 s. Each variant's exe and dll were copied into the game folder before its run.
- Save: Castle Town Market by the fountain. Scenes: `kokiri-idle` (standing), `castle-move` (walking and turning), `hyrule-walk` (the street to the gate; samples and profile only).

## Variants
- base: Sync 10 (upstream's 849 hot functions and profile, loop-header polls).
- h2: 2949 hot functions (+917 from our samples, +1183 small callees), with a profile from our three scenes merged 6:1 with upstream's.
- h2o3: h2 at `RX_HOT_OPT=O3`.
- p2: h2's list, upstream's back-edge polls (0d6ed03), with a profile recorded on that code.
