## Desktop check (for the user: is the 66 ms stall session 0's?)
Launch two runs from a normal desktop session (double-click a .bat in `C:\OOT\game` or a cmd window, not ssh), the Market save,
standing in the castle-town Market for ~2 minutes each, with `RX_VK_FRAME_TRACE=1` and `HOS_SPEED_LOG=1` (the 4 default variables of
the bench harness otherwise):
- A (default): nothing extra.
- B (run-ahead): `set RX_VK_LATE_MERGE=1`; optional B2: `set RX_VK_CAPTURE_DEFER=1` and `set RX_VK_CAPTURE_SLACK=1000000`.
Look at: fps (`bench.json` or the overlay) and the frame times in `hookshot_frames.csv` (`frame_ms`, `late_wait_ms`, `gpu_gap_ms`,
`gpu_up_ms`): A should show ~9 ms of `late_wait_ms` and a steady ~35 ms frame; B should show `late_wait_ms` near 0 and either steady
24-26 ms frames (the stall is session 0's: take it further) or frames snapping to ~66 ms with `gpu_gap_ms` 25-50 (the stall is real).
Also check B's picture for white screens with only the HUD (seen here) and the speed ratio (`vi: speed`, ~60.0).
