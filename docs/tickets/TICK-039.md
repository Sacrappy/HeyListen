# TICK-039 — The game's job-group wait: critical path, wake latency, per-job overhead  · model: Opus
**Why**: TICK-037: ~80% of a Market frame is the guest CPU chain; the main thread waits 6.4-7.1 ms a frame for its job group (f_8007fe60 / WaitLightEvent) while the job threads (BaseProcExecutor0-5, ModuleSystemWorker1-5, NavMeshCAStepDepWorker1) are only 12-50% busy. So the group's time is a dependency chain, not total work: what lies on it (job execution, guest->host wake latency of light events / condvars / SleepThread polls, recompiled-code overhead like obj_lookup, page marks, indirect calls, locks)?
**Goal**: reconstruct the job graph's critical path per frame (job start/end per thread, who signals whom, wait->wake latency), attribute it, and cut it: faster wake paths for guest sync primitives (spin-then-block, futex/WaitOnAddress, thread priorities/affinity), lower per-call overhead in the lifted code, or native replacements (TICK-026 framework) of the hottest jobs on the path. Each behind a switch, picture and game speed unchanged.
**Acceptance**: breakdown of the job-group wait; measurable fps gain in kokiri-idle/castle-move beyond the 2-4 fps spread, or proof and a ranked plan.
