# TICK-023 — VRAM saturation  · model: Opus · priority
**Why**: the user's session (RTX 5070, 12 GB) reached 11.6 / 11.9 GB in the overlay; the game log shows "VRAM: 9829 MB used of 10935 MB allowed", textures ~5.6 GB against a 6144 MB texture budget with evictions, mirrors up to 1.4 GB, 40+ rendered 4096x4096 textures (32 MB each). One rendering corruption (dark sky, black silhouettes) happened once on the Hyrule Field -> Kokiri Forest transition and could not be reproduced: likely VRAM pressure at an area change. TICK-015 keeps mirrors that hold left-out write-backs forever ("never given back").
**Goal**: account for VRAM by category over a session with area changes (textures by kind: decoded ASTC, rendered, kept finalized; mirrors; pools; pipelines; recording streams; swapchain/upscaler), find what grows or is kept without need, and fix: (1) TICK-015 mirrors: write back then free when a pool is retired/unused instead of keeping forever; (2) texture budget from VK_EXT_memory_budget (what the OS allows, minus a margin) instead of a fixed 6 GB; evict least-recently-used before allocations fail; (3) release textures/rendered targets of the previous area on scene change; (4) any duplicate copies (e.g. ASTC decoded + BC3 re-encoded both kept).
**Acceptance**: VRAM peak and steady-state reported before/after on a run with at least one area transition (reload of the save counts; Hyrule Field <-> Kokiri if a scripted path exists); peak well under the device budget (target < 80%); no corruption (screenshots across the transition); fps unchanged.
**Branch**: `feature/TICK-023-vram`
