# TICK-030 — Teal ground / missing textures after TICK-023 evictions  · model: Opus · TOP PRIORITY (regression)
**Symptom** (user, 2026-11-09, main build with TICK-023, Castle Town Market): the ground renders as a flat teal surface and the end of the street as a cyan glow; walls, NPCs, HUD fine. Overlay 54 fps, VRAM 9.6/11.9 GB.
**Evidence** (user's E:\OOT\hookshot-fork-game\hookshot_log.txt at 16:42): "VRAM breakdown" lines show "ASTC decoded" textures dropping from 2269-3692 MB to 72-87 MB while "free in VMA blocks" is 4.8-4.9 GB and heaps 84-93%; "textures the GPU drew, written back into their memory before eviction: 151 so far". TICK-023 changed: texture budget from the driver (80% of allowance minus the rest, free-in-blocks counted up to 512 MB; refused images lower it to 7/8), big images (>= 16 MB) in dedicated memory, render targets evicted after 600 unused presents when over budget.
**Hypothesis**: the new budget is far too low (it counts the whole process, mirrors 1.8 GB at times, and VMA free space) so the LRU evicts in-use textures every frame; a terrain array / ground texture evicted and re-created wrong or late (or rendered targets evicted then reused without their contents) gives the teal ground.
**Goal**: reproduce (Castle Town save = most recent; force pressure with RX_VK_VRAM_TARGET_PCT low if needed, compare with the pre-TICK-023 build backup-tick023), find the exact failing path, fix it so in-use textures are never evicted (evict only textures unused for N frames; never shrink the budget below what the current frame uses), and make the budget sane (count VMA free space as reusable).
**Acceptance**: no teal ground in Castle Town across 3 runs with screenshots + one run under forced pressure; VRAM no worse than pre-TICK-023 peak; fps unchanged.
**Branch**: `feature/TICK-030-eviction-regression`
