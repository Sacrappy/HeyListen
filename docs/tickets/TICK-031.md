# TICK-031 — VRAM: fit the game in a Switch-2-like budget  · model: Opus · priority
**Why**: missing / green-ground meshes reappear when VRAM is full (user screenshots: 11.6 / 11.9 GB on a 12 GB RTX 5070, Castle Town). The Switch 2 runs the game in 12 GB of shared LPDDR5X total; we use ~10 GB of VRAM plus ~15 GB of system RAM. Known causes of blow-up: ASTC textures (natively sampled on the console) decoded to larger formats on PC (RGBA8 = 4-8x an ASTC 8x8 block's size; BC3 re-encode 2x-4x), every guest memory pool held twice (host RAM + VRAM mirror), rendered targets and finalized copies kept, mirrors of pools in use (~1.3-1.8 GB). TICK-023/030 tuned budgets and eviction; this ticket reduces what is needed.
**Goal**: (1) exact VRAM accounting by source over a Castle Town move run (decoded ASTC by target format and size class, rendered targets, mirrors by pool, staging, pipelines); (2) cut the biggest: ASTC -> smallest acceptable BC format per content (BC7 for colour 8bpp, BC1 4bpp where alpha-free and quality allows, BC4/BC5 for 1-2 channel data, keep mips; re-encode on worker threads with a disk cache), mirror only the ranges the GPU uses (sparse mirrors / smaller pool granularity), share or drop finalized duplicates; (3) when VRAM is still short, degrade safely (never draw a mesh with a missing/teal texture: wait or use a lower mip, log it).
**Acceptance**: peak VRAM at Castle Town move and Hyrule Field down by >= 30%; no missing meshes or teal/green ground in screenshots during moves (headless shots); fps not lower; image quality checked side by side for the re-encoded textures.
